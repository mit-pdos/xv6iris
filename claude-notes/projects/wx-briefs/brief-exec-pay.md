=== THE DESIGN ===
X1 THE SLOT WANDS TAKE THE PAYLOAD.  `SpecKexec.exec_slot_pre`'s two wands (the loadable arm and the other-success arm) conclude `Q (-1) -∗ S W'` where they concluded `S W'` -- i.e. they additionally take the exec'ing process's payload at status -1, which is what the new image's run holds as its own `ukn_pay N' (-1)` (the exec keeps the generation, so `my_pay (uvis_gen W') Q` already pins the new run's `ukn_pay N' = Q`). Every supplier adapts: `exec_au_pre_triv_at`/`UexecExecMint.uslot_mint`/`UexecCond.cond_entry_slot`/`InitBoot.init_boot_bundle_triv` at the trivial payload take `True` (or `Q (-1)` at `Q = fun _ => True`) and hand it to `uslot_of_urun_all`; `PinnedExec.pex_slot`/`pinned_exec_bundle`'s constructor wand (`… -∗ my_pay (uvis_gen W') Q -∗ Pay -∗ X W'`) gains `Q (-1) -∗` beside `my_pay`; `UInitSh`, `UShKernel.sh_uexec_slot`/`sh_slot_of_kexec`, `UInitKernel`, `USyncKernel`, `UEchoKernel` take it and pass it to `uslot_of_urun_all` instead of `done` (the trivial ones still discharge trivially at `Q = fun _ => True`, but STATE the parameter so sh's non-trivial `Q` can flow).
X2 THE KERNEL HANDS IT.  `SpecSyscall.sysc_exec_out`'s success arm becomes `sexit_pay f (-1) -∗ uslot (uvis_of U' sts' gn cs pid)` (the payload the dispatcher holds from `sysc_pay_in` is what the slot needs; on the FAILURE arm the process resumes at its old key and gets `sysc_pay_out` as today); `SpecUsertrap.ut_exec_out` the same; the dispatcher's exec arm (`ProofSyscall`) builds the wand from kexec's post (`exec_post_ok_recv` gives the slot wand's application -- with X1 the received slot piece is `Q (-1) -∗ uslot …`, so the arm is `sysc_pay_in`'s `sexit_pay f (-1)` fed to it) -- decide whether `sysc_pay_out` still appears unconditionally in the post or only on the arms that resume the old key; PREFER: the returning post keeps `sysc_pay_out` on every arm EXCEPT the exec-success arm, whose `uslot` has consumed it (state it as the disjunction the arm already is); the trap loop's exec arm (`ProofUserretClosed`, `UexecApply.uexec_ret_round_slot`'s exec case) feeds `uexec_pay_arm f` to the new slot's wand instead of the old key's continuation; `uexec_arm_F`'s exec case follows.
