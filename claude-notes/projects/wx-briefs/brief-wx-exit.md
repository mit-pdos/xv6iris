=== THE DESIGN ===
X1. THE KERNEL'S QUARTER AND THE PAY FACT LIVE IN THE PRIVATE BLOCK.  `ProcInv.proc_priv_core` gains `∃ Q, gen_kq (pv_gen V) pa pid Q ∗ my_pay (pv_gen V) Q` (the quarter is linear, the pay fact persistent; `pa`/`pid` are `proc_priv`'s own parameters). kfork's split (`kfork_arm3`) no longer drops the quarter: it and `my_pay` go into the CHILD's block as it is assembled (the child's slot premise keeps receiving `my_pay γ Q`, now a second copy -- persistent); userinit's assembly of init's block splits allocproc's full ownership at `Q := fun _ => True%I` (userinit has no parent: `child_tok` is dropped there; state it). X2. THE EXIT DEPOSIT.  `exit_xs (tf : list (mword 64)) : Z` := the signed 32-bit reading of `tf_arg_idx 0` (what `p->xstate = status` stores; state it once in UexecSlot or UexecRet). The trap loop's exit row (`uexec_dep_F`/`uexec_ret_F`'s `if decide (n = USYS_exit) then emp`) becomes the DEPOSIT `∃ Q, my_pay (uvis_gen W) Q ∗ Q (exit_xs (uvis_tf W))` (the arm stays `emp`: exit does not return). The route carries it: a new `ut_exit_in`/`sysc_exit_in` row beside `ut_sys_in`/`sysc_sys_in` (which exclude exit), threaded to `SpecSysExit`/`SpecKexit`, whose premise gains the deposit. kexit at its ZOMBIE store: from its block's `gen_kq (pv_gen V) pa pid Q` and the deposit's `my_pay (pv_gen V) Q' ∗ Q' xs`, build the ESCROW and park it: `park_pay ZOMBIE`/`proc_dormant_noctx` gains `exit_tok (pv_gen V) pid xs` with `xs` = the `p_xstate` cell's value (`proc_pub`'s `p_xstate pa ↦₄ xs`: tie them -- state `⌜xs = exit_xs …⌝` or key the escrow at the cell's value; the reaper reads the cell). Re-cut `ChildTok.exit_tok` if the agreement needs the deposit's own `Q'` inside: `exit_tok γ pid xs := ∃ pa Q Q', gen_kq γ pa pid Q ∗ my_pay γ Q' ∗ Q' xs` and `gen_pay` redeems via agreement of `child_tok`'s Q with either (a ▷ appears once; `gen_pay_timeless` gives ◇).
X3. REPARENT MOVES THE CHILDREN TO INIT'S ORPHANS.
