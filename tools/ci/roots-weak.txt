# The top theorems of the weak-memory tier Weak/ (the Weak twin of
# tools/ci/roots.txt).  One fully qualified name per line.  Weak/ is not part
# of the Xv6/MachCSL build that CI, the coverage / dead-code reports
# (tools/ci/envfacts) and the default assumption audit load, so these roots
# are kept apart: they are audited by `tools/ci/audit.sh --weak` against
# tools/audit/baseline-weak.json, and tools/ci/lint.sh fails if the two lists
# differ.  Two families: the AxSpin allocpid theorems and certificates
# (AxSpinT2*), and the allocproc plan's INTERNAL milestone M1
# (Weak.srvwmoXv6M1Core, SrvwmoXv6Adequacy; NOT published [OWN.2]).
