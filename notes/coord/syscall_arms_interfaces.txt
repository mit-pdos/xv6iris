1. THE CONTRACT (SpecSyscall.wp_syscall_body; D32 eb-generic)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  binders: PT (park token, SchedNames → IProp GF, persistent -- abstract until W8-P2), Γ [ClaimIs Γ],
    cpu k γw γ j pid V M sts gn cs ip (f : UexecSG.sfam GF); hj hproc (k.proc = procAddr j)
    hK (syscallSlots ≤ k.avail) hnoff (k.noff = 0) htier (k.tier = kpt) hgn (gn = V.gen).
    SYSCALL additionally binds [∀ Γ, Persistent (PT Γ)] and [UexecSG GF] (the deposit class; W8-K's instance).
  pre:  kctx cpu k ∗ pcIs cpu syscallAddr ∗ procsInv Γ ∗ trapCsrsExt cpu k.sie ∗ cpuClaimExt cpu k.sie k.proc
        ∗ isLock γw waitLockAddr "wait_lock" waitLockPay
        ∗ bslots 3 ∗ syscInitId ip ∗ fdSlots FDSPARE ∗ irefSlots IREFSPARE      (the four explicit families)
        ∗ syscallEnv PT Γ γ
        ∗ procPrivFd γ (procAddr j) pid V M ∗ fdFrags V.fdg sts ∗ chFrag V.chg (procAddr j) cs
        ∗ syscSysIn f V M sts gn cs pid ∗ syscForkIn f V M sts ∗ syscPayIn f V
        ∗ (wpNext true k.proc cpu (syscallPost PT Γ k γ j pid V M sts gn cs ip f) ∧ syscallCloser k V)
  post (syscallPost, at every hart): ∀ spie spp R' V' M' sts' cs',
        ⌜calleeSaved k.regs R'⌝ -∗ ⌜SyscRows V M V' M' sts sts' cs cs' pid⌝ -∗
        kctx cpu' ((k.withSpie spie spp).withRegs R') -∗ pcIs cpu' (jumpPc (k.regs 1#5)) -∗
        trapCsrsExt cpu' k.sie -∗ cpuClaimExt cpu' k.sie k.proc -∗ <the 4 families> -∗ syscallEnv PT Γ γ -∗
        procPrivFd γ (procAddr j) pid V' M' -∗ fdFrags V.fdg sts' -∗ chFrag V.chg (procAddr j) cs' -∗
        syscExecOut V M V' M' sts sts' gn cs pid -∗
        syscSysOut f V M sts gn cs pid (syscA0 V') (syscImg V' M') sts' V'.cwi cs' -∗
        syscForkOut f V (syscA0 V') cs cs' -∗ syscWaitOut V M (syscImg V' M') (syscA0 V') cs cs' pid -∗ wpLoop cpu'
  closer (syscallCloser): stackOwn k.sp (trapRes k.sie + k.avail) -∗ stackOwn (V.kstack + 4096#64) 512
  syscImg V M := umemLazy V.upt V.sz.toNat M   (the key's lazy image);  syscA0 V := tfW V.tf (tfArgIdx 0).

  syscallEnv PT Γ γ = syscProcEnv Γ γ ∗ consoleReadyApp ∗ syscFsEnv ∗ firstDone ∗ parkWorld Γ ∗ PT Γ
    syscProcEnv Γ γ = ∃ γp γw γft γtk, nextpid lock ∗ procsAvailAt Γ none false ∗ wait_lock ∗ isFtable γft γ ∗ isTickslock γtk
    syscFsEnv       = panicEnv ∗ fsReady
    parkWorld Γ     = (∃ ports/locks/pages, devintrCaps Γ … fscDisk fscDlock …) ∗ consoleReadyApp ∗ nextpid lock ∗
                      procsAvailAt Γ none false ∗ wireInv ∗ kmapAt trampVpn (kLeaf trampPpn .rx 0 0) ∗
                      (∃ ip, initIdentCell curCtx ip ∗ initGen ip 1)
  Projections (all `syscallEnv PT Γ γ ⊢ …`): _console _first _world _token _fsReady _panic _all _ftable _ticks
    _pid _kmem (at fscKalloc/fsReadyKmem, none) _devsw (∃ γl γu, filewriteDevsw γl γu);
    builders: _filereadFsEnv (⊢ bslot -∗ …) _filestatFsEnv (bslot) _filewriteFsEnv (bslots 3)
    _fileclosePipeEnv (procsInv Γ -∗ fileclosePipeEnv Γ fscKalloc fsReadyKmem none)
    _filecloseFsEnv j hj (procsInv Γ -∗ bslots 3 -∗ filecloseFsEnv Γ j (procAddr j))
    _fsFabric (procsInv Γ -∗ ∃ pd pav pu, fsFabric Γ pd pav pu).
  The environment is persistent ONLY under [∀ Γ, Persistent (PT Γ)] -- every arm theorem binds
  `(PT : SchedNames → IProp GF) [hPT : ∀ Γ, Persistent (PT Γ)]` so `#Henv` works.

    kctx cpu (((k.withSpie spie spp).pushed 4).withRegs R) ∗ pcIs cpu (syscTarget n) ∗
    frame4s2 (k.regs 2#5) (k.regs 1#5) (k.regs 8#5) (k.regs 9#5) (k.regs 18#5) ∗
    procsInv Γ ∗ trapCsrsExt cpu k.sie ∗ cpuClaimExt cpu k.sie k.proc ∗ wait_lock γw ∗

    syscSysIn f V M sts gn cs pid ∗ syscForkIn f V M sts ∗ syscPayIn f V ∗
    (wpNext true k.proc c0 (syscallPost …) ∧ syscallCloser k V)  ⊢ wpLoop cpu
  (c0 = the dispatch's entry hart, the post's key; syscall_post_at turns it into any hart since k.proc ≠ 0.)

   5. build `SyscRows V M (syscStore V1 (R2 10#5)) M1 sts sts' cs cs' pid` (syscRows_keep for entries that
